- Each run contains the original 12 terrain columns: total 26,352 robot episodes.
- Forward F01/F02 and sine A2/wavelength16, A1.5/wavelength16 are retained; diagonal/lateral left and right alternate across conditions/seeds.

- No outcome-dependent mixture optimization or 1:1:1:1-versus-3:1:1:3 comparison belongs in the final paper.
- Same initial/physical state and terrain between Normal and Student; original pair hashes preserved.
- Normal22500 uses the privileged physical-information Teacher encoder. Student77500 uses the history-based Student path. This is a comparison of the complete policy configurations.

METRICS
1. Survival: binary non-fall fraction, fall=0, goal or administrative cap without a fall=1. NOT normalized mean survival time.
2. Motion commands: per-trial valid post-fault vx/vy/yaw-rate RMSE, then average trials. m/s,m/s,rad/s.
3. Path tracking: cross-track RMSE perpendicular to the independent world-frame reference path. m.
- No zero padding after falling. Zero valid samples -> NA. Compare errors with survival and valid observation length.
- Stall flag: new maximum route progress less than 0.5 m over 30 s. No early termination for stalls or slow tracking.
- For diagnosis, report stagnation among survivors with an explicit survivor denominator; transient stall and noncompletion are different records.
